# iOS

Read for iPhone interface behavior. For shared iPad code, load the iPad reference only when the task includes it.

Decision prompts:

- Does the navigation hierarchy match the task? Distinguish switching sections, pushing detail, and presenting a temporary task.
- Can people reach primary actions and dismiss presentations through familiar controls? Keep gesture-only actions discoverable and accessible.
- Does content remain usable with the keyboard visible, larger text, safe-area changes, and the supported display sizes?
- Do loading, empty, error, and permission-denied states leave a clear next action?

Look up the relevant component through [Designing for iOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ios) and the [HIG index](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines). Use [interface guidance](interface.md) for layout/accessibility questions. Do not transfer Mac window or menu expectations to iPhone.
